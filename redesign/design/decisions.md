# Decisions

## Open proposals (for the owner)
- **P1 Level names: decided 2026-10-19.** The four bands stay as in the inventory:
  - 85+ **Highly Recommended**
  - 65–84 **Recommended**
  - 45–64 **Considered**
  - under 45 **Not Recommended**

  "Need a closer look" (F7) = Considered + Not Recommended. The direction mockups still use the old placeholder names; the prototype uses these.

- **P2 Rebranding for other client companies (not in the brief yet).** Owner asked how the design would hold up if the product were duplicated for other companies. Proposed:
  - one brand theme (brand colour, mark, name) over fixed product colours;
  - the verdict band uses the brand colour only when white text passes contrast (3:1 large, 4.5:1 small), otherwise a deep neutral band with the brand colour as an accent.
  - This copy is that rebrand: the "PT Example" theme in `tokens.css`, with cool neutral greys in place of the original warm ones.

- **Q1 Behaviour bands: decided 2026-10-19.** Competency scores fall in three bands, each with its behaviour description: under 50 **Needs Improvement**, 50–74 **Average**, 75 and over **Strong**.
- **Q2 Saving a PDF offline: decided 2026-10-19.** The PDF is made on the device, so Save PDF works offline. The "needs a connection" state for saving is gone.
- **Q3 "Preview the PDF": decided 2026-10-19.** Keep it: a link next to Save PDF that opens the A4 pages on screen.

## Direction choice
- **2026-10-19, after round 1:** owner asked for a mix based on **B Scoreboard**, because A and C felt too generic. The designer recommended B as the most distinctive and most like the client's own tool. Mix = B minus the decorative circle and the blue level dot, plus C's board grouped by level as the home page and C's "biggest strength, biggest gap" pair.
- **2026-10-19, final pick: D Scoreboard mix.** Owner's reason: "Looks quite cool". It keeps B's distinctive, on-brand verdict band, which the owner preferred over the more generic-feeling A and C. Polish (insight typography, the PDF, one motion moment, stress data, states) moves to stage 2.
