# Looks the design approves on purpose; the fresh-eyes checker is told not to report them

- The native Role picker overlays the board's headings and candidate rows while open; the design specifies native selects (design.md §3, Filter pills).
- The PDF preview preserves A4 proportions and fits the whole page on phones. Fine print is small at this overview scale; live browser pinch zoom and the real-text PDF render it sharply (design.md §5). The named zoom capture shows a panned portion of the paper at 3×.
- Gallery files named `before-*` deliberately preserve historical defects for comparison with `after-*`; they do not depict the current UI.
